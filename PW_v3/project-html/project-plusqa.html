Enterprise cloud storage platform (100M+ users)

I work on an enterprise cloud storage platform used by over 100 million people. Most of my time goes to test automation — writing the automated checks and setting up what they run on. At this scale a regression does not affect a handful of users, it affects millions of people's files, so the point of automation is less about speed than consistency: the same checks run the same way on every build.

I also write and maintain the test cases that define what coverage means for my area, and keep them current as features change. A lot of that coverage sits below the interface. API testing lets me check behaviour at the service layer before it reaches a client, and I spend a good share of my time on the data and backend side, confirming that what the system stores and returns is actually correct. On a storage product that is the whole job — the screen can look fine while the data behind it is wrong.

PLUS QA

PLUS QA is a software quality assurance company, founded in 2008 in Portland, Oregon. It has a team of 70+, all based in the US, and works with 100+ clients from a lab of 600+ real devices. It is a minority-owned business. The work covers functionality, device compatibility, accessibility, usability, localization and API testing, plus automation and managed testing, across web, mobile, AR/VR, IoT and e-commerce.

Joining PLUS QA pushed me further into the technical side of QA. My earlier roles were mostly exploratory testing, investigation and documentation. Here the focus is automation, coverage design and the layers underneath the interface, which is the direction I wanted to grow in.
