march 7th 2023 [personal project / game jam]

gat-cha fish.

A game inspired by the idle-game category at the time.
My main vision for this project was a game you'd put on the background, and when you heard a little ping that's when you'd know you caught a fish.
You'd quickly go back to the game and press the correct sequence of buttons to reel the fish in. The goal was to simulate a more realistic fishing experience.

Unfortunately I didn't do a good job of explaining the initial concept of it and this was recieved poorly because people thought it was a a very-active game. When it was actually the opposite.
Which taught me how I need to make these game explanations a lot more clearer, as looking back I can definiltey understand their perspective.

- THERE IS NO SAVE STATE FOR THIS GAME
- Game can only be played on 700px or wider

how to play

Keep the game open and make sure your sound is turned on. From time to time, you'll hear a small ping, which means a fish has been hooked!

When you hear the ping, quickly mash the W key to start reeling the fish in. Then, use the arrow keys and follow the on-screen prompts to reel it in successfully. The game will display which arrow key to press as you play.

Once you've caught a fish, you can view it in the Fish Collection by pressing 2.

You can also purchase a fish for $50 by pressing Y. To win the game, earn $1,000 and then buy the final item by pressing U.

While in the Fish Collection scene, press P to open the menu and view your total number of fish. To sell a fish, hover over it and press O.
