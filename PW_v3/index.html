resume [06/18/26]

I am a Junior Game/Software Developer and QA Analyst with over three years of experience in the video game and software industry. My journey began in the competitive scene, specializing in platform-fighters, auto-chess, and 2D fighting games, and has since evolved toward designing engaging game systems, developing software, and ensuring quality across product releases.

Currently, I'm developing a mobile chess game — a from-scratch Unity variant with a custom move-generation and ability-resolution engine powering fairy pieces like the Berserker and the area-blast Mine, unlockable through a gacha-style rarity system. Networked online multiplayer is next on the roadmap.

I am working at PlusQA as a Jr. QA Lead/QA Engineer, supporting a large-scale cloud storage platform. My work involves creating test plans and validating software quality across product releases, ensuring every update meets a high standard before it reaches users.

Documentation and relevant code samples for my work can be found within their respective project repositories on my GitHub.
