I am a Junior Game Designer and Developer with over two and a half years of experience in the video game and software industry. My journey began in the competitive scene, where I specialized in platform fighters, auto-chess, and 2D fighting games. Over time, my focus has evolved toward the creative and technical side of development—specifically designing systems and mechanics that deliver balanced, engaging gameplay.

Currently, I am dedicated to developing a custom chess project alongside a desktop journal application, focusing on refining user experience and core mechanics.

To further expand my technical foundation, I am attending Portland Community College to study Microelectronic Technology. My goal is to bridge the gap between high-level software design and the physical hardware that powers it, gaining a "foundry-to-screen" perspective that allows me to build more efficient, high-performance digital experiences.

Documentation and relevant code samples for my work can be found within their respective project repositories on my GitHub.
