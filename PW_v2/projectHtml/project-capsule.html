glhf - capsule v0.5

how to play:

Each character is valued at $100 and belongs to one of four tiers, with higher tiers generating greater passive income over time. The ultimate objective is to purchase "THE KING". Players can also sell characters or rename them for a more personalized experience.

In the sell menu, green text indicates how much the character earns per cycle and the amount you’ll receive when selling them. Red text displays the time (in seconds) it takes for the character to generate income.

The core gameplay is simple: watch your characters roam the world as they generate income, purchase more characters, and work your way toward affording the final, ultimate character. While a lot of planned content had to be cut for this version, I aim to include it all in the final release to create a more complete and engaging experience.

- THERE IS NO SAVE STATE FOR THIS GAME
- Game can only be played on 700px or wider (No Mobile)


about: glhf - capsule v0.5

This project was inspired by my recent interest in idle games, particularly titles like Gnorp Apologue and Tower Wizard. I wanted to create a simple idle game focused on collecting a variety of characters. What I’ve developed so far is a bare-bones prototype—essentially a version 0.5—representing the foundation of what I hope to build in the future.

During development, I ran into a major challenge: I wasn’t able to implement a key feature early on, and trying to add it later would have required rebuilding the entire system from scratch. As a result, several planned features had to be cut from this version. Despite that, I learned a lot from the process, and moving forward, my vision for the full project is much clearer.

In future iterations, I plan to integrate NFC tag functionality—allowing players to unlock characters by scanning physical keychains containing those tags. I’ll also be designing small 3D models to print as keychains, creating a fun link between real-world collectibles and in-game content. The long-term idea is to let players scan a character’s unique tag to bring it to life digitally, blurring the line between physical and virtual gameplay.

Before working on version 1, I have a few other projects I want to complete. One is a small app that connects to the Steam inventory system to enable item drops. Another is a productivity-focused study app designed to help users stay off their phones. I also plan to create a basic 3D platformer to begin learning 3D game development more seriously.

There’s a lot I want to accomplish, and while balancing work and development time can be tough, I’m committed to pushing these ideas forward. I’m planning to share progress updates here through journal posts, both to track my growth and to document the development journey.
