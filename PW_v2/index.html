resume [09/20/25]

I'm a Junior Game Designer and Developer with over two years of experience in the video game industry. My journey began as a competitive player in platformer fighters, auto-chess, and more recently, 2D fighting games. Over time, my focus has evolved toward the creative and technical aspects of development—particularly in designing game systems and mechanics that deliver engaging, balanced gameplay experiences. I'm currently expanding my skills with the goal of creating a 3D platformer, with a strong emphasis on fluid movement and responsive controls.

In my free time, I’m expanding my skill set by learning 3D modeling with the goal of producing custom resin figures. Inspired by vinyl figure artists who design and produce their own collectibles, it has long been a personal ambition to create a system where people can collect both a physical figure and a corresponding digital asset. By combining what I'm learning in Unity, Blender, and JavaScript, I'm actively working toward building a prototype to bring this concept to life.

I’m currently working as a Test Associate II at Experis, where I’m assigned to Meta. I’m part of the team supporting the Horizon Worlds project, contributing to quality assurance and testing efforts to ensure a seamless user experience.
